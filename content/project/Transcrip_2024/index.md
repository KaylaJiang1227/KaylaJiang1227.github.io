- Investigated how iron (Fe) and phosphorus (P) availability affect photosynthesis and gene regulation patterns in *Arabidopsis thaliana*.  
- RNA-seq experiment conducted on 7-day-old seedlings:  
  - **Control group (C1–C3):** +Fe+P (normal condition)  
  - **Treatment group (T1–T3):** –Fe+P (iron deficiency, phosphate present)  
- Analyzed differential expression between control and treatment groups to identify key pathways involved in nutrient deficiency-induced chlorosis.  
- Provided insights into molecular mechanisms underlying Fe–P interactions and their roles in photosynthetic regulation.
